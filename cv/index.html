Research profile

Siefeldin Sobih

I’m integrating vision-language models into SLAM algorithms, working on how robots share observations and link the same objects across their maps.

Logan, Utah

Research experience

Graduate Research Assistant

2026-01 — Present

Utah State University · Department of Mathematics · Kevin Moon Lab

  • Working on swarm robotics, integrating vision-language models into SLAM algorithms.
  • Integrating vision-language and large language models into robot-to-robot communication so robots can share and interpret observations without a central controller.
  • Adapting algorithms that merge object annotations across robots’ maps so observations of the same object can be linked without duplicate entries.

Graduate Research Assistant

2023-08 — 2025-12

Utah State University · Department of Biology · Karen Kapheim Lab

  • Analyzed gut microbiome and health data in R using mixed-effects models and multivariate tests to assess relationships between health measurements and microbiome composition.
  • Developed a regression model to predict Accu-Chek readings from i-STAT measurements, with a cross-validated MAE of 9.0 mg/dL and RMSE of 12.8 mg/dL.
  • Automated data-analysis workflows with Nextflow and adapted them for a high-performance computing cluster.
  • This work led to two pending publications on endangered rock iguana subspecies.

Intern Research Assistant

2021-01 — 2021-05

Nile University - Bioinformatics

  • Developed branching logic for a Python bioinformatics pipeline that checks dependencies, prompts users to select tools, passes their inputs through Bash commands, and collects the output. This work contributed to a published paper.
  • Developed a Python script to retrieve PAM250 scores for aligned amino acid pairs between humans and other species, replacing repetitive manual matrix lookups.
