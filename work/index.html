Swarm robotics: VLMs and SLAM

Integrating vision-language models into SLAM algorithms and robot-to-robot communication.

RoboticsVLMsSLAM
About this work +

In Kevin Moon’s lab at Utah State University, I’m working on how robots share and interpret observations without a central controller. I’m adapting algorithms that merge object annotations across their maps so observations of the same object can be linked without creating duplicate entries.

Research2023–2025

Rock iguana microbiomes

Studying how seasonality and human activity relate to the microbiome and physiology of endangered Bahamian rock iguanas.

MicrobiomesRNextflow
About this work +

Analyzed gut microbiome and health data using mixed-effects models and multivariate tests. Developed a regression model to combine glucose readings from two instruments, and automated analysis workflows with Nextflow for an HPC cluster. The work led to two pending publications.
