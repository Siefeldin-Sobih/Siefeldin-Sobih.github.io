## Start with a question

Use this space to introduce an idea, share an observation, or write about something you are trying to understand. A post can be short. It does not need to be a polished essay.

## Follow the thought

You can add headings, links, lists, and pictures. The page keeps a narrow reading column so the writing stays comfortable to read.

- Begin with what caught your attention.
- Add the example or experience behind it.
- Leave the reader with a question or a next step.
